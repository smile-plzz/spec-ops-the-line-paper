## 2. Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

### Inclusion Criteria (IC)
- **IC1 (Relevance):** Direct, substantive analysis of *Spec Ops: The Line* (narrative, mechanics, ethics, player experience, or cultural representation) OR foundational theoretical/philosophical works directly applicable to the core argument (e.g., procedural rhetoric, ethical game design, unreliable narration, militainment, Orientalism).
- **IC2 (Authority & Rigour):** Peer-reviewed journal articles, scholarly book chapters, academic monographs, conference proceedings from established bodies (e.g., DiGRA, ACM CHI), or verified primary source accounts by lead creative personnel (writers, directors, narrative designers).
- **IC3 (Methodological Integrity):** Works that provide traceable evidence, sound textual or empirical methodology, and verifiable bibliographic records.
- **IC4 (Language):** English-language publications or official English translations.

### Exclusion Criteria (EC)
- **EC1 (Superficiality):** Commercial consumer reviews, gameplay walkthroughs, surface-level wiki summaries, or brief journalistic news blurbs lacking critical or analytical depth.
- **EC2 (Unverifiability):** Anonymous forum posts, Reddit threads, unverified fan theories without scholarly peer review or primary textual grounding.
- **EC3 (Methodological Fabrication):** Studies purporting to present empirical human-subject findings without institutional ethics disclosures, sample methodology, or peer review.
- **EC4 (Pure PR/Marketing):** Pre-release marketing press releases that do not contain reflective developer analysis.

## 4. Search and Screening Synthesis

- **11 peer-reviewed articles and conference papers** directly analyzing *Spec Ops: The Line*.

- **5 verified primary developer accounts** (interviews, GDC presentation, narrative design postmortem, and memoir).
- **12 foundational theoretical works** in game studies, moral philosophy, literary narrative theory, postcolonial geography, and empirical game psychology.
- **2 canonical intertextual references** (*Heart of Darkness*, *Apocalypse Now*).

All sources have been verified for publication metadata, peer-review status, and factual citation integrity.
