# Project Context & Scope

## Academic Investigation
**Title:** Do You Feel Like a Hero Yet? Narrative Deception, Moral Complicity, Psychological Deterioration, and the Ethics of Play in Spec Ops: The Line

### Research Scope & Constraints
- **Subject:** *Spec Ops: The Line* (2012), developed by Yager Development, published by 2K Games. Written by Walt Williams and Richard Pearsey; lead design by Cory Davis.
- **Disciplinary Boundaries:** Game Studies (Primary), Media Studies, Narrative Studies, Psychology of Interactive Media, Moral Philosophy, Cultural Studies.
- **Citation Format:** APA 7th Edition.
- **Manuscript Target Length:** 7,000–9,000 words (excluding references & appendices).
- **Core Research Questions:**
  - Primary RQ: How does *Spec Ops: The Line* use narrative deception, character deterioration, conventional military-shooter mechanics, constrained player agency, and psychological manipulation to challenge the player's understanding of heroism, responsibility, and complicity in digital violence?
  - Sub-RQs: RQ1 (Narrative construction & inversion), RQ2 (Character arcs & deterioration), RQ3 (Taxonomy of deception & manipulation), RQ4 (Mechanics as instruments of moral complicity), RQ5 (Affective and psychological reception), RQ6 (Developer intentions vs. finished text), RQ7 (Cultural and ideological critique of Dubai, race, gender, and militainment), RQ8 (The ethics of interactive responsibility under forced progression).
- **Tooling Stack:**
  - Python 3.13 (`python-docx`, `pandas`, `requests`, `pillow`)
  - Git version control
  - Academic Research Skills Pipeline (v3.24.1)
  - Web & academic indexing engines (Crossref, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, DiGRA, Game Studies)
